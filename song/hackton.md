## Slide 10 — You can approach it by choosing one or more of the two branches

Either building data infrastructure or developing services that solve regional problems using that data.

### 1. Building Data Infrastructure
Public data related to Gyeongsangbuk-do (or those helpful to Gyeongsangbuk-do administration) is processed and disclosed in a format that AI can immediately use, such as MCP servers, open-source skills, and plugins, in a standardized manner.

### 2. Regional Problem-Solving Services
Freely discovering and analyzing various regional issues in Gyeongsangbuk-do using open data and AI, and developing AI-based services and solutions to solve regional problems.

**Constraint** — At least one public data piece required. If you choose to build infrastructure, you must specify your open-source license and publish it on GitHub.

---

## Slide 11 — Submissions must meet these conditions

Don't just process or release it — please also submit a prototype you can actually feel it with.

| 항목 | 설명 |
|---|---|
| **Public Data Required** | Must utilize at least one public dataset, such as data from the Public Data Portal |
| **Ready to Run** | Solutions should be easy to deploy and use, even for people with no prior knowledge. |
| **Open Source Contribution** | Teams working on data infrastructure projects must publish their code on GitHub and clearly specify the license. |
| **Prototype Bonus** | Additional points will be awarded for tangible deliverables such as chatbots, web services, mobile apps, or live demos. |

**Avoid** — Simple UI mockups without actual data integration, exposing original data without processing

## Slide 12 — Judging is based on five criteria, weighted and evaluated

From technical sophistication to open-source impact, we look at whether what you create can actually be used.

| Criteria | Description | Weight (%) |
|---|---|---|
| **Technical Excellence** | Solution quality, technical soundness, and effective use of AI and public data | 25% |
| **Public Value & Applicability** | Potential to address real public needs and create measurable local impact | 35% |
| **Innovation & Differentiation** | Originality, creativity, and uniqueness compared to existing approaches | 25% |
| **Sustainability & Scalability** | Long-term viability, maintainability, and potential for broader adoption | 15% |
| **Prototype Bonus** | Additional credit for working demonstrations such as chatbots, web apps, dashboards, or services | 10% |
